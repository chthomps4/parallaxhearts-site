# Design comparison and intentional deviations

The image concepts establish visual direction; they are not screenshots or a claim of owner-approved pixel specifications. The owner delegated implementation decisions and subsequently selected **The Golden Section** as the anchor. That explicit instruction takes precedence over the earlier homepage concept's collection action.

Reviewed the generated references and actual Chromium captures at their native 1487×1058 concept viewport. Also inspected the final desktop and phone pages. Full responsive evidence covers 375, 430, 768, 1024 and 1440 pixels; see [the evidence gallery](../review-evidence.md). The implementation uses HTML/React/CSS text and controls, not flattened mockup images.

| Comparison point | Reference | Implemented result and reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Homepage composition | [Home concept](home-concept.png): oversized two-line collection title on the left, orbital moon on the right, thin copper rules | [Actual first screen](../screenshots/after/home-first-screen.png): same hierarchy, split composition, orbital artwork and rules. Header and actions use practical live-site sizing; the reading band begins a little lower than the concept. |
| Anchor and actions | Original concept leads to the collection; later [anchor concept](anchor-concept.png) introduces a dedicated poem feature | The main action reads “Read The Golden Section.” The poem feature immediately follows the hero on Home and Crown. This is the owner's requested content hierarchy. All actions have real destinations. |
| Palette and image treatment | Midnight, moon white, copper and warm paper | Flat midnight background, copper rules and actions, pale text, and parchment reading surfaces are retained. The standalone lunar asset has an edge mask to blend its boundary; no full hero tint is applied. Texture is deliberately quieter than the generated paper concept. |
| Typography | Large literary serif display, italic phrase, monospaced poem excerpt | Locally licensed Cormorant Garamond and DM Mono reproduce the type family and hierarchy. Georgia supports prose/navigation. Live text is smaller than some image-concept labels to accommodate real controls, longer titles and comfortable line lengths. No image lettering substitutes for poem text. |
| Anchor artwork and whitespace | Moon, construction lines, spiral and ordinary folding chair on paper | [Actual anchor feature](../screenshots/after/anchor-section.png) retains those subjects and generous blank space. It uses a separately generated square illustration with its own line placement and chair drawing rather than extracting the concept. It is identified as new artwork, not an original supplied poem card or a mathematically exact construction diagram. |
| Additional poetry | [Poetry concept](poetry-concept.png): open editorial entries and excerpts | [Actual poetry band](../screenshots/after/poetry-section.png) uses open columns and rules. Four additional approved entries follow the dedicated anchor. The later twelve-poem submission expands the original two-poem concept; the archive holds all fourteen approved works. |
| Explorations | [Explorations concept](explorations-concept.png): dark editorial section with two clear topics | [Actual section](../screenshots/after/explorations-section.png) retains the type contrast, open spacing, copper divisions and two destinations. The interior geometry page adds an accurate SVG construction and controls; healing copy distinguishes tradition from available evidence. |
| Existing work | Initial concept shows a generated street scene | The implemented music/story feature deliberately uses the repository's existing Vallen photograph. Existing music, story, book and visual-novel material remains accessible. Archive interiors retain their distinct design instead of being replaced by illustrative placeholders. |
| Responsive behavior | Desktop concepts have no fully specified phone layout | [Actual phone page](../screenshots/after/home-375.png) stacks reading before art, preserves generous spacing, and uses a keyboard-operable menu. No horizontal overflow was found at the five tested widths. Native mobile share sheets and physical devices remain outside the completed emulation scope. |
| Publication details | Concepts omit real metadata, filters, errors and editorial states | Actual pages add search/filter controls, empty states, related poems, canonical/share metadata and accessible focus. Local review is explicit and private to loopback; current approved poems do not show a held-content warning. These functional additions are intentional. |

The result follows the references' literary direction while accommodating the owner's later anchor choice and approved collection. It does not claim exact pixel reproduction or approval of a particular rendered mockup. Original uploaded poem-card file bytes remain unavailable; the site preserves the approved HTML transcriptions and provides a workflow for importing those originals later.
