# Crown of Phases — 18 New Poem Drafts

**All poems are new writing for owner review.** They are not previously published poems, and they must not be auto-published.

Quantum and astronomical references are observations or metaphors, not claims that human experiences obey quantum entanglement, astrology or unverified frequency-healing laws.

## The Good Scissors
**Theme:** Feminine Sovereignty · **Slug:** `the-good-scissors`

```text
Her mother kept
the good scissors
in the back of a drawer
beneath the tea towels.

“For fabric,” she said.
“Nothing else.”

Years later
her daughter took them out
to shorten the sleeves
of a coat she loved
but never wore.

The blades met cleanly.

She went outside
before the thread
was put away.
```

**Editorial intent:** A woman stops saving the good tools and the good coat for an unspecified occasion.
**Visual direction:** Old dressmaking scissors on linen, partly altered coat sleeve, a loose spool of rust-colored thread; no portrait.

## The Way Back
**Theme:** Feminine Sovereignty · **Slug:** `the-way-back`

```text
She left the party
before they cut the cake.

In the car
she kept both hands
on the steering wheel
until the song ended.

She was fond of them.
She was glad to go home.

Both things fit
in the same small car.
```

**Editorial intent:** Affection and the wish for solitude can coexist without either being false.
**Visual direction:** Parked car seen from above on a quiet residential street, warm party windows far behind.

## The Sleeve
**Theme:** Connection & Desire · **Slug:** `the-sleeve`

```text
She leaned across the table
to take a piece of bread.

Her sleeve slipped back.
I lost the end
of what I was saying.

“Finish,” she said,
still holding the bread.

I tried.

She was kind enough
not to help.
```

**Editorial intent:** A flash of attraction in an everyday conversation, with an affectionate comic turn.
**Visual direction:** Intimate still life of bread, two plates and a rolled-up linen sleeve, warm restaurant table light.

## The Compass Point
**Theme:** Sacred Geometry · **Slug:** `the-compass-point`

```text
The compass needle
made a hole
in the page.

Around it
I drew a circle
so exact
I forgot
the little damage
at its center.

Later
I held the paper
to the lamp
and found the opening
again.
```

**Editorial intent:** The almost invisible cost of making an exact shape becomes the image.
**Visual direction:** Close-up cream drafting paper and brass compass, tiny pinhole illuminated by a warm desk lamp.

## The Hexagon
**Theme:** Sacred Geometry · **Slug:** `the-hexagon`

```text
I copied a honeycomb
onto graph paper,
every angle tidy,
every cell
the same.

Then I left
a narrow white gap
between the shapes
to make the drawing
look less crowded.

Mine was easier
to frame.

The bees
had been making room
for honey.
```

**Editorial intent:** Geometry looks different when function matters more than the beauty of the diagram.
**Visual direction:** Macro honeycomb pattern adjacent to a slightly imperfect penciled hexagonal grid on paper.

## Before Eight
**Theme:** Moon & Cosmos · **Slug:** `before-eight`

```text
The moon was still up
when the school bus came.

Someone near the back
tapped the window.

By the next corner
three small faces
were pressed
to the glass.

The driver
kept his eyes
on the road.
```

**Editorial intent:** A daylight moon draws attention during an otherwise ordinary school morning.
**Visual direction:** Pale daytime moon above a quiet suburban road and a distant bus, no identifiable children.

## The Telescope Cap
**Theme:** Moon & Cosmos · **Slug:** `the-telescope-cap`

```text
I drove past
the last grocery store
to photograph Saturn.

Set up the tripod.
Checked the focus.
Adjusted the exposure.

Ten minutes later
I discovered
the telescope cap
was still on.

Some nights
the universe
is not the difficult part.
```

**Editorial intent:** Technical care is sometimes undone by an extremely human oversight.
**Visual direction:** Portable telescope on a dark roadside beneath the stars, cap resting on eyepiece, no faux planet compositing.

## The Surprise
**Theme:** Moon & Cosmos · **Slug:** `the-surprise`

```text
She checked her horoscope
while the kettle boiled.

“Expect a surprise,”
it said.

At seven
the neighbor’s cat
came through
the screen door
with a leaf
stuck to its tail.

She checked the time.

Fair enough.
```

**Editorial intent:** A gentle collision between cosmic prediction and domestic comedy.
**Visual direction:** Kitchen kettle, warm lamplight, a cat near a screen door with one leaf on its tail.

## Error Bars
**Theme:** Science & Uncertainty · **Slug:** `error-bars`

```text
At the science museum
a chart showed
each measurement
with a small line
above and below.

I liked that
even the careful numbers
made room
for being wrong.

On the train home
I took out my phone
and changed the message
I had been so certain about.
```

**Editorial intent:** Scientific uncertainty offers a practical reminder to revise certainty about people.
**Visual direction:** Hand-drawn measurement chart with visible uncertainty intervals beside an unbranded phone on a train table.

## The Unmeasured Minute
**Theme:** Science & Uncertainty · **Slug:** `the-unmeasured-minute`

```text
I tried to work out
exactly when
the evening changed.

Perhaps when
the waiter took
the second empty plate,
or when we stopped
checking our phones.

I wrote down
the time we left.

That was the only
certain number.
```

**Editorial intent:** An attachment to measurement meets a moment that cannot be timed cleanly.
**Visual direction:** Closing restaurant table with two empty plates and a small handwritten time on a receipt.

## The Tuning Fork
**Theme:** Sound & Resonance · **Slug:** `the-tuning-fork`

```text
I struck the fork
against the rubber block.

It sounded thin
in the open room.

Then I touched its stem
to the wooden table.

The note grew larger
without changing its pitch.

For a moment
I forgot the explanation
and listened.
```

**Editorial intent:** An acoustic demonstration makes room for wonder without replacing the physics.
**Visual direction:** Tuning fork touching a worn wooden tabletop, warm copper reflections, restrained sonic ripples as ornament only.

## Room Tone
**Theme:** Sound & Resonance · **Slug:** `room-tone`

```text
The recorder started
before anyone spoke.

Refrigerator hum.
A chair leg
moving over tile.
Rain beginning
at the window.

I labeled the file
“silence”
and kept it.
```

**Editorial intent:** A recordist notices all the small sounds inside what is casually called silence.
**Visual direction:** Compact field recorder on a table near a window, rain-speckled glass and blurred chairs.

## Breath Count
**Theme:** Mindfulness & Care · **Slug:** `breath-count`

```text
I tried to count
ten breaths.

At four
the radiator knocked.

At seven
a dog next door
announced a catastrophe.

I lost my place.
Began again.

The dog
appeared to be
doing his own practice.
```

**Editorial intent:** Meditation as an ordinary, imperfect attempt rather than a promise of transformation.
**Visual direction:** Quiet room with a radiator and open notebook, distant dog-shaped silhouette beyond a window.

## After the Appointment
**Theme:** Mindfulness & Care · **Slug:** `after-the-appointment`

```text
She came home
with a list of things
she could not fix
by wanting them fixed.

She put the list
under the fruit bowl,
peeled an orange,
and called a friend.

The conversation
did not cure anything.

It made the kitchen
less lonely.
```

**Editorial intent:** Care is sometimes companionship rather than a cure; avoid implying a treatment outcome.
**Visual direction:** Orange peel curling near a fruit bowl, folded paper beneath it, softly lit kitchen, no medical props.

## Two Tickets
**Theme:** Connection & Desire · **Slug:** `two-tickets`

```text
They bought separate tickets
to the same show.

Met in the lobby
under a clock
five minutes slow.

Afterward
one took the bus
and one walked home.

The song
was still playing
in both their heads.
```

**Editorial intent:** Connection that matters without requiring the people to merge their routes.
**Visual direction:** Vintage concert stubs on dark wool, lobby clock out of focus; two diverging nighttime streets.

## One at a Time
**Theme:** Science & Uncertainty · **Slug:** `one-at-a-time`

```text
The physicist showed us
a screen recording
one photon at a time.

The first dot
told us almost nothing.
The fiftieth
didn't settle it.

Only after many more
did the bands
begin to show.

I wrote that down
without pretending
it explained
anyone I loved.
```

**Editorial intent:** A quantum interference demonstration illustrates how a pattern emerges from many events; the poem rejects using physics as evidence about human relationships.
**Visual direction:** Photographic dots accumulating into bands on a monochrome projection screen, a notebook margin nearby; avoid depicting people as physically entangled.

## A Table of Phases
**Theme:** Moon & Cosmos · **Slug:** `a-table-of-phases`

```text
At the kitchen table
she marked the moon
on the calendar.

A thin arc.
A half-circle.
The full round face.

Then a week
of crossed-out errands
and grocery lists.

When she looked again,
the moon had changed
without consulting
the margins.
```

**Editorial intent:** The lunar cycle proceeds whether or not anyone keeps up with their plans.
**Visual direction:** A paper calendar with hand-drawn moon phases, grocery notes and erasures, moon faintly visible beyond kitchen glass.

## The Wrong Word
**Theme:** Writing Process · **Slug:** `the-wrong-word`

```text
The first draft
had “infinite”
in three places.

I crossed it out twice.

On the third
I wrote “until Tuesday”
and the poem
finally knew
where it lived.
```

**Editorial intent:** An editorial decision trades inflated abstraction for a specific and believable time.
**Visual direction:** Actual-feeling handwritten draft with visibly crossed-out “infinite” and penciled “until Tuesday”; no imitation of existing notebook.
